    The visualizations below represents data on number of world heritage sites across three Scandinavian countries [Norway (NO), Denmark (DK) and Sweden (SE)] at two different time points (2004 and 2022).

    https://100.datavizproject.com/data-type/viz10/

    The visualization above emphasizes progress among the countries, including how DE surpassed NO. However this visualization can be considered bad for a number of reasons.
